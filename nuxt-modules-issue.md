A Nuxt 3 module for vue-player — a feature-rich video player with HLS streaming, subtitles, chapters, and thumbnail previews. The module auto-imports VideoPlayer and usePlayer across your entire app with zero config.

Features:
- Auto-imports VideoPlayer component (no manual imports needed)
- Auto-imports usePlayer composable for programmatic control
- Injects player styles globally
- HLS adaptive streaming support (via optional hls.js)
- Subtitles / closed captions (WebVTT)
- Chapters support
- Thumbnail preview on timeline scrub
- Configurable component prefix
